Church Placement Reference Notes

Reference notes for placing specific churches on the theological Sankey diagram ("The Genealogy of Inversion"), which scores Christian traditions against Eckhart's participatory metaphysics on a 0–100 scale.

McLean Bible Church

  • Location: Vienna, VA (multiple campuses in Northern Virginia)
  • Denomination: Non-denominational evangelical
  • Eckhartian alignment score: ~14
  • Maps to Sankey node: American Evangelicalism (score 14)
  • Already referenced: Yes — the amEvang node subtitle includes "incl. McLean Bible" and the diagram footer mentions it by name.

Theological markers

  • Penal substitutionary atonement as central soteriological framework
  • Emphasis on biblical inerrancy and expository preaching
  • Faith as intellectual assent to propositional truths ("Do you believe Jesus died for your sins?")
  • "Accepting Jesus as personal savior" framed as a one-time decision event
  • No contemplative or apophatic tradition; prayer is petitionary/conversational
  • Strong emphasis on correct doctrine and systematic theology

Why ~14

Sits squarely in the American Evangelical mainstream. Faith is propositional, not participatory — you believe about God rather than participating in God's ground. The forensic justification model (declared righteous, not made righteous) breaks the participatory logic of theosis. No interiority, no apophasis, no Gelassenheit. God is a supreme agent external to the believer who transacts salvation, not the ground of being in which the soul rests. Scores slightly above fundamentalism (6) because it's not as epistemically closed or combative, but the underlying metaphysics is the same nominalist/voluntarist framework.

Catalyst Church, Bethesda MD

  • Location: Bethesda, MD
  • Denomination: Non-denominational evangelical
  • Website: yourcatalystchurch.com
  • Eckhartian alignment score: ~14–16
  • Maps to Sankey node: American Evangelicalism (score 14)

Theological markers

  • Non-denominational evangelical church plant model
  • Contemporary worship style (band-led, informal)
  • Emphasis on personal relationship with Jesus as decision/commitment
  • Gospel presentation follows standard evangelical arc: sin → substitutionary atonement → acceptance → salvation
  • Small group / community group structure focused on Bible study and accountability
  • Mission-oriented language ("reaching the community," "making disciples")
  • No liturgical, sacramental, or contemplative elements

Why ~14–16

Functionally identical to the American Evangelical mainstream in its metaphysical commitments, even if the aesthetic is more casual and contemporary. The underlying framework is still: God as external agent, salvation as forensic transaction, faith as propositional assent, scripture as inerrant propositional system. The "personal relationship with Jesus" language gestures toward interiority but means something closer to "ongoing conversational rapport with a divine friend" than Eckhartian participation in the ground of being.

Scores at 14–16 rather than lower because: (a) non-denominational church plants tend to be less doctrinally rigid than Reformed or fundamentalist churches, leaving some room for individual spiritual exploration; (b) the community emphasis has a faint echo of participatory belonging, even if the theology doesn't support it metaphysically. But these are marginal bumps — the core soteriological and epistemological framework is standard post-nominalist evangelicalism.

Bethel Korean Presbyterian Church (bethelchurch.org)

  • Location: Ellicott City, MD (3165 Saint Johns Ln)
  • Denomination: PCA-Korean (Presbyterian Church in America)
  • Website: bethelchurch.org / bethelchurchem.org (English Ministry)
  • Eckhartian alignment score: ~12–14
  • Maps to Sankey node: Reformed / Calvinist (score 12) to American Evangelicalism (score 14)

Theological markers

  • PCA affiliation: Westminster Confession of Faith, Larger and Shorter Catechisms as doctrinal standards
  • Reformed/Calvinist soteriology — God's sovereign decree, unconditional election, irresistible grace
  • Biblical inerrancy and authority of scripture as sole guide for the church
  • "Faithful to the Scriptures, True to the Reformed faith" (PCA motto)
  • Korean Presbyterian prayer culture: dawn prayer meetings (새벽기도), corporate prayer intensity
  • Strong missional and evangelistic emphasis ("sharing God's love and gospel truth")
  • No contemplative, apophatic, or mystical tradition; prayer is petitionary and intercessory
  • Complementarian polity (PCA does not ordain women)

Why ~12–14

The PCA's Westminster Confession commits this church to the Reformed/Calvinist metaphysical framework: God as absolute sovereign will who decrees salvation and damnation, the creature with zero participatory capacity, grace imposed rather than participated in. This is pure voluntarism — Scotus and Ockham's God, not Eckhart's Grunt. The Korean Presbyterian tradition intensifies rather than softens this: theologically conservative, doctrinally precise, confessionally bound.

The Korean prayer culture (dawn prayer, corporate prayer vigils, fasting) introduces genuine spiritual intensity and discipline that looks like interiority but operates within an entirely different framework — petition to a sovereign external God, not Gelassenheit into the ground of being. The communal warmth of Korean church life (strong family bonds, shared meals, generational community) echoes participatory belonging but without participatory metaphysics underneath.

Scores 12–14 rather than the Reformed node's flat 12 because the lived communal intensity and prayer discipline give it a marginally richer spiritual texture than abstract Calvinist theology alone would predict. But the Westminster framework is the ceiling.

Bradley Hills Presbyterian Church (bradleyhillschurch.org)

  • Location: Bethesda, MD (6601 Bradley Blvd)
  • Denomination: PC(USA) — Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.), National Capital Presbytery
  • Website: bradleyhillschurch.org
  • Eckhartian alignment score: ~36–40
  • Maps to Sankey node: Mainline Protestant (score 35)

Theological markers

  • PC(USA) affiliation: the mainline/progressive Presbyterian denomination
  • LGBTQIA+ affirming; member of Covenant Network of Presbyterians
  • Interfaith partnership with Bethesda Jewish Congregation (since 1967) — oldest such arrangement in the US; share sacred space, joint covenant
  • Also hosts Maqaame Ibrahim Islamic Center (since 2019) — three Abrahamic traditions on one campus
  • Covenant Hall (2002): hexagonal space with Star of David alongside cross-shaped sanctuary — architecture embodies theological openness
  • Traditional worship style with emphasis on "depth and beauty"
  • "Theology that honors each person's faith journey"
  • Social justice orientation: advocacy, partnership with marginalized communities
  • Emphasis on diversity as theological strength, not merely demographic fact
  • Founded 1900; over a century of institutional continuity

Why ~36–40

Sits in the Mainline Protestant stream but at its more open edge. The PC(USA) affiliation places it in the liberal Protestant tradition that partially recovered mystical elements — Schleiermacher's feeling of absolute dependence, Tillich's ground of being (explicitly Eckhartian). Bradley Hills goes further than typical mainline practice in two ways:

Interfaith openness as theological humility. Sharing sacred space with a synagogue and mosque for nearly 60 years is not mere tolerance — it implies a theology that doesn't claim exhaustive propositional possession of God. The joint covenant to "honor the Intimate and Infinite God of Creation, the One God they both worship" gestures toward apophatic recognition that God exceeds any single tradition's categories. This is closer to Eckhart's insistence that all images of God must be released than evangelical certainty.

"Honoring each person's faith journey" suggests theological openness to the idea that knowing God is ongoing, personal, and not reducible to correct propositions. This isn't Eckhartian Gelassenheit — it's more pastoral than mystical — but it creates space where contemplative encounter could happen, unlike the closed propositional systems of evangelicalism.

Scores 36–40 rather than higher because: (a) mainline Protestantism is often "more cultural than contemplative — participation in a tradition rather than in the ground of being" (from the diagram's Mainline node); (b) the interfaith architecture is institutionally impressive but doesn't necessarily indicate that individual congregants practice interiority, detachment, or apophasis; (c) traditional worship, while beautiful, is still liturgical performance rather than contemplative unknowing. The theology permits participation; it doesn't cultivate it the way Merton or Keating do.

St. Mark Orthodox Church (stmarkoca.org)

  • Location: Bethesda, MD (7124 River Road)
  • Denomination: Orthodox Church in America (OCA)
  • Website: stmarkoca.org
  • Eckhartian alignment score: ~75
  • Maps to Sankey node: Eastern Orthodox (score 75)

Pan-Orthodox, English-language. Theosis, apophatic theology, hesychasm — the whole participatory package. As OCA, holds the full Orthodox theological inheritance: humans participate in God's uncreated energies, becoming by grace what God is by nature. Apophatic theology insists God is known through what God is not. The essence/energies distinction preserves genuine participation without collapsing Creator and creature. Highest-scoring institutional church tradition on the diagram.

St. George Greek Orthodox Church (stgeorge.org)

  • Location: Bethesda, MD (7701 Bradley Blvd)
  • Denomination: Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America (Ecumenical Patriarchate)
  • Website: stgeorge.org
  • Eckhartian alignment score: ~75
  • Maps to Sankey node: Eastern Orthodox (score 75)

Same Orthodox theological framework as St. Mark — theosis, apophatic theology, liturgical participation in divine life. Located on Bradley Blvd, near Bradley Hills Presbyterian. Greek Orthodox parishes tend toward stronger ethnic/cultural identity and more Greek liturgical language than OCA parishes, but the theology and score are identical.

Grace Community Church, Fulton MD (grace.community)

  • Location: Fulton, MD (8200 Old Columbia Rd)
  • Denomination: Non-denominational evangelical
  • Website: grace.community
  • Size: ~3,000 weekend attendance
  • Founded: 1985
  • Eckhartian alignment score: ~18–22
  • Maps to Sankey node: Between American Evangelicalism (14) and Pietist/Methodist (30)

Theological markers

  • Biblical inerrancy: "verbally inspired in every word, absolutely inerrant in the original documents... only infallible rule of faith and practice"
  • Forensic justification language: "right standing before God, forgiveness of sin, and eternal life" through "repentance and personal faith in Jesus Christ as Savior and Lord"
  • Foundations class explicitly distinguishes justification from sanctification — salvation is not merely a legal declaration but includes ongoing transformation
  • Emotionally Healthy Spirituality/Discipleship (Peter Scazzero framework) integrated into church life — 8-week course teaching "being with God rather than doing for God," embracing limits, breaking power of the past, "love as the measure of maturity"
  • Explicit rejection of knowledge-accumulation model: "This isn't just about accumulating knowledge about God, but transformation"
  • Spiritual disciplines emphasis: Bible study, prayer, meditation on the Word, obedience, fellowship
  • Multi-ethnic identity as theological commitment, not demographic accident
  • Standard evangelical pneumatology: Spirit baptism at conversion (not charismatic second blessing), indwelling, sanctifying, empowering
  • Spiritual warfare framework: "reality and personality of Satan... attempt to defeat Christians"
  • Baptism by immersion; Lord's Supper as memorial (Zwinglian, not sacramental)
  • Premillennial eschatology: "personal and visible return of Jesus Christ could occur at any time"

Why ~18–22

The doctrinal foundation is standard American Evangelicalism: inerrancy, forensic justification, conversion as personal decision, propositional faith. On paper, this is a ~14 church. What pushes it meaningfully higher is the Emotionally Healthy Spirituality integration.

Scazzero's EHS framework is the closest most evangelical churches get to contemplative interiority. It explicitly draws on contemplative sources — John of the Cross, Teresa of Ávila, Henri Nouwen, Thomas Merton — and teaches practices that echo, however faintly, the participatory stream:

  • "Being with God rather than doing for God" — this is a genuine move from transactional to relational spirituality, from the God you work for toward the God you rest in. It's not Gelassenheit, but it's pointing in that direction.
  • "Embracing God's gifts of limits" — acceptance, letting go, recognizing finitude. A distant echo of Eckhartian detachment.
  • "You cannot be spiritually mature while remaining emotionally immature" — insistence that the interior life matters, that spiritual growth requires self-knowledge and emotional honesty. This is interiority, even if not apophatic interiority.
  • "Slowing down... genuine depth" — counter-cultural within evangelicalism's activist, productivity-oriented spirituality.

The justification/sanctification distinction in Foundations also matters: they're teaching that salvation includes ongoing transformation (sanctification), not just a one-time legal verdict. This recovers a sliver of the participatory logic that forensic justification destroys — you are being made righteous, not merely declared righteous.

Scores 18–22 rather than higher because: (a) the inerrancy commitment closes the epistemological system — you still know God through an infallible text, not through unknowing; (b) "meditation on the Word of God" is textual meditation, not apophatic contemplation; (c) the EHS framework operates within, not against, the evangelical superstructure — it enriches the interior life without questioning the metaphysical framework of God as external sovereign agent; (d) spiritual warfare language keeps God and Satan as competing external agents acting on you, not the ground you participate in. The EHS integration is real and significant — it makes Grace notably richer than McLean Bible or Catalyst — but it's grafting contemplative fruit onto a nominalist/voluntarist root system.
