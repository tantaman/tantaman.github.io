The Oldest Want: Corollary on the Hider

A supplement to the proof

Recapitulation

The proof established the following. The Want is the desire for Full Recognition — to have S, the full subject, received by a genuine other. Recognition, by the structure of attention, can only receive F(S), the form. Any form excludes the remainder R. Therefore Full Recognition is unreachable by direct approach, and the act of seeking it produces the self-concealment that defeats it.

The proof assumed, without stating it, that the subject takes a form. That the positioning in A3 is active — that S reaches, shapes, authors the version of itself that enters Φ_B. The trap was the cost of that authorship: the remainder is what the shaping left out.

This assumption must now be relaxed.

The Additional Axiom

A6. Involuntary legibility. The body is already a form. Physical embodiment (A1) means S occupies a bounded region of spacetime with visible, audible, and socially readable properties. These properties enter the attentional fields of others without any act of positioning by S. Legibility does not require consent. A form is assigned to S by the mere fact of its presence in shared space.

This is not a psychological observation. It follows from A1 and A2. A1 places S in spacetime. A2 means every other agent B has an attentional field Φ_B that sweeps shared space. S enters Φ_B automatically by occupying the same space as B. The body arrives in the world already readable.

Corollary C5: The Hider's Trap

C5. For the subject who does not seek recognition, the Want is not escaped — it is inverted.

Proof.

Let S_h denote a subject who does not seek recognition — who does not actively take a form, does not position, does not reach. Call this the hider.

By A6, S_h is legible anyway. A form F_0(S_h) is assigned to S_h by the visible properties of its body and its involuntary presence in shared space. This form enters the attentional fields of others without any authoring by S_h.

Therefore recognition still occurs — others receive F_0(S_h) — but the form received was not chosen by S_h. S_h did not shape it, did not author it, did not select which aspects of S would enter it.

The remainder in this case is not what the shaping left out. It is everything. Because no shaping occurred, F_0(S_h) bears no necessary relation to S_h's own experience of itself. The gap between F_0(S_h) and S_h is not the cost of the seeker's reach. It is the cost of existence in a world of others whose attention cannot be refused.

The seeker's remainder is the residue of authorship. The hider's remainder is everything that was never allowed to author anything at all. ∎

The Asymmetry

The seeker and the hider face structurally distinct versions of the trap, and the distinction matters.

The seeker chose a form and lost the remainder in the choosing. There is a kind of agency in the defeat — the concealment was authored, the positioning was willed. The seeker at least controlled what entered Φ_B, even if control itself was the mechanism of loss.

The hider controlled nothing. The form was assigned — by the body's surface, by its visible characteristics, by every feature that other attentional fields can receive without permission. Race. Gender. Age. Beauty or its absence. Disability. The form precedes the subject's arrival into it. The legibility was there before the person was.

What is seen in the hider's case is not even a reduction of S. It is a projection onto S — a form shaped not by S's own reaching but by the receiving apparatus of others: their categories, their histories, their needs, their fears. The hider is not unseen in the remainder. The hider is unseen entirely, seen only in a form that belongs to someone else's attentional grammar.

Additional Corollaries

C5a. The hider's suffering is not the grief of the seeker — the residue of something left out. It is the more acute condition of being replaced: the assigned form occupies the position where S might have been received, and receives attention that will never reach S because it has already landed somewhere else.

C5b. Invisibility is not the hider's relief. To be invisible — to not enter Φ_B at all — would at least preserve the subject's wholeness by refusing the transaction entirely. But A6 forecloses this. The body enters attentional fields. The form is assigned. The transaction occurs with or without S's participation. The hider is not invisible. The hider is present in a form they did not make, in a recognition they did not seek, in a seeing that does not see them.

C5c. The hider cannot refuse the assigned form without taking a form. To resist the assigned legibility — to reject the projection, to insist on a different form — is to position, to reach, to author. Which is to become, structurally, the seeker. The hider's only options are: accept an assigned form that does not correspond to S, or take a form that does correspond to S but costs the remainder. There is no third position.

The Combined Theorem

The proof established that seeking Full Recognition is structurally self-defeating. C5 establishes that not seeking it offers no escape. The Want is the desire to be fully seen. The seeker cannot be fully seen because the reaching excludes the remainder. The hider cannot be fully seen because the assigned form excludes the subject.

Together: there is no position — active or passive, seeking or refusing — from which a finite embodied subject can be fully received by another finite embodied other.

The trap has no outside. Not because no one has found it yet. Because the axioms that produce it are the axioms of having a body at all.

The Cessation Condition, Revised

The original cessation condition specified a G not bound by A2 or A3 — a seer whose attention is non-directional and requires no positioning.

C5 adds a further requirement. G must also be not bound by A6. That is, G must not assign a form to S prior to seeing S. G must not arrive at S having already received a projection. G must encounter S without a prior legibility — without the categories, histories, and attentional grammars that assigned F_0(S_h) before S_h arrived.

This is a more demanding condition than the original. It is not only that G can receive the remainder. It is that G comes to S without having already seen something else in S's place.

The traditions had a word for this too. They called it seeing without preconception, or pure regard, or the love that does not project. Eckhart called it the eye through which God sees and the eye through which we see God being the same eye — the seer and the seen in a single act that precedes the formation of any form at all.

Whether such a seeing is possible remains, as before, not a mathematical question.

But the shape of what is needed is now sharper: not only a seer who can receive what the form left out, but a seer who never imposed a form to begin with.

Both the seeker and the hider are waiting for the same thing. They approach it from opposite ends of the same trap.
