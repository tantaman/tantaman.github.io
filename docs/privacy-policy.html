Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-02-19

What We Collect

When you send a message to us via WhatsApp, we collect:

  • The text content of your message
  • Your phone number (as provided by WhatsApp)
  • The timestamp of your message
  • The WhatsApp message ID

How Messages Are Used

Messages you send are stored in our database and may be used as content on this site.

Publication

Messages you send to us via WhatsApp may appear publicly on tantaman.com. We may edit or excerpt messages before publishing. By sending us a message, you acknowledge that it may be published.

What We Don't Collect

We do not use cookies for tracking purposes. We do not run ad networks. The only third-party analytics service used on this site is Google Analytics (see below).

Data Retention

Messages are stored indefinitely unless you request deletion.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your messages at any time by contacting us. To do so, send a WhatsApp message requesting deletion, or email matt@tantaman.com.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as page views and referral sources. Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact matt@tantaman.com.
