# Iran: The Resolution — Timeline of Predictions

## 2026 — The War

- Khamenei dead, Islamic Republic collapsing under US-Israeli bombardment *(premise — already happening)*
- Hezbollah supply lines permanently severed
- Iranian navy and missile infrastructure destroyed
- Trump claims credit, seeks deal framework with provisional Iranian counterparty

## 2026–2028 — The Immediate Aftermath

- Persian monarchist exile movement consolidates political voice
- Provisional post-theocratic government emerges in Iran
- Gulf states (Saudi, UAE, Bahrain, Egypt) complete Abraham Accords normalization with Israel
- Pro-Palestine solidarity movements at peak intensity in Western capitals
- China begins positioning for reconstruction contracts
- Erdogan (or successor) makes first major bid to speak for the ummah in the post-Iranian vacuum

## 2028–2035 — The Transition

- "Persia" identity restoration underway — secular nationalist rebranding of Iran
- Exile community begins returning
- Chinese state-owned enterprises building ports, railways, power grids across the new Persia and wider Gulf
- Religious settler movement expands into southern Lebanon
- Turkey emerges as primary institutional home for Muslim political anger
- Sunni renarration takes hold: Islamic Republic as "Shia deviation," not true Islam
- Shia populations (Iraq, Lebanon, Bahrain, Pakistan) revert to quietist posture — endurance, not revolution
- Palestinians marginalized below political threshold — wound persists, voice does not
- Western pro-Palestine solidarity redirects toward Iranian women's liberation, new sacred order

## 2035–2050 — The New Order Consolidates

- Turkey consolidates recognized leadership of the Sunni world
- Third Temple construction begins in Jerusalem
- Belt and Road integration pulls China into full economic entanglement with Abrahamic powers
- The long peace is experienced as genuine — generational, structural, not just diplomatic
- Christianity begins significant growth phase in China, underground church surfaces
- Founding violence (the bombardment of Iran) renarrated as liberation in Western and Sunni media, taught in schools

## 2050–2070 — The Synchronization

- China reaches ~30% Christian population
- Chinese Christianity develops indigenous dispensationalist movements
- China's eschatological impulse migrates from exhausted/failed nationalist narrative into Christian framework
- China fully legible to Abrahamic sacrificial cycle — assignable prophetic roles
- Abrahamic triangle (Judaism, Christianity, Islam) operating in unprecedented tripartite peace
- Mythic canopy at full strength — the generation that witnessed the founding violence still alive

## 2070–2100 — The Canopy Thins

- Last generation with living memory of the Iran war dies
- Dispensationalist hermeneutic engine begins quietly remapping: Meshech and Tubal as Anatolian/Turkish
- "Kings of the East" commentary tradition gains traction re: China
- Turkey, now a confident Muslim civilizational bloc, increasingly cast as next prophetic obstacle
- Shia and Palestinian memory — transmitted through families for decades — begins to find political voice again as the sacred order's legitimacy weakens

## 2100–2150+ — The Cycle Returns

- Turkey or China identified as next scapegoat by the re-formed coalitional logic
- The peace recognized, in retrospect, as having been built over a fault line
- The mechanism repeats: community in crisis, convergence on a victim, violence as resolution
- Or: someone reads the text again with fresh eyes

---

*Note: These timeframes are approximate and represent the essay's structural logic, not point predictions. The thesis is that the mechanism operates on generational timescales (~80–150 years for the full cycle). Individual events may accelerate or delay, but the sequence is what the essay stakes its credibility on.*
