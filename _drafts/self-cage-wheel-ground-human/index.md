Our end is dictated by our desires. Our desires are dictated by our environment. A man is weak. The quickest way to change is to change his environment. To require will power for every act is to never accomplish anything. To be surrounded by temptation that will never let you rest.

We recognize this privately. Change your peer group. Change your habits. Don't buy things that tempt you. Leave the phone somewhere else.

We no longer recognize this socially. Or, if we do, we have limited control. You play russian roulette with your children. Sending them to schools and daycares, hoping they don't fall under the influence of a crazy-eyed sociopath.

Maybe we believe we're above it. That once we've lived enough will, at some point, reach a glorious peak where we can survey all that has happened and choose who we are. This is a flattering myth of who you are. You must be a God if this is you. Or blind.

Even if you are a God, is everyone capable of being a God like you? Or shall we give them environments where their path is not dogged by temptation?

The body can clearly be forever damaged by its environment. To believe the mind is not like this is another god like belief.

---

Once we accept that we are made great by great environments, we see that we can no longer act individually to reach great heights. The wealth parents who pick private schools, move to good school districts, curate play dates, limit screen time know and do not know this.
They know, on the one hand, because they're curating their environment.
They do not know, on the other hand, because they think this-choice-in-the-market is enough.

They do not see that their choices have already been arranged into a menu. And that the menu only features destitute items.

There is no infinite menu so choices are limited.
The menu itself may be a menu of trash despair than a menu of joy.
If all menus are menus of despair, one will never realize the possibility of a menu of joy.
