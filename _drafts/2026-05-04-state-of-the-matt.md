Here we have arrived. At what have we arrived? At a new understanding of the world. Of the structures I am in. Of no longer being dumbfounded. Having moved from a child's certainty to an adolescent's confusion to a young man's certainty in uncertainty and dissolution to an older man's certainty in certainty and structure.

The key phrase that continually sticks with me:

> "Post-modernism flipped the table only to find all the pieces falling right back into place."

That the structure of what is is not to be found by reading what is given to us by unknown narrators but only through one's body, local community, and accepted-as-honest record.

The work experiences that stick with me:

> To be a successful engineer requires frameworks and ways of thinking. Reflecting on experience and mistakes. Beginning with the end in mind. The ability to stop and focus and work through things in pain staking detail as paramount. But also the ability to fly through everything all at once and to see the trend and to get ahead of it. That work was a testing ground, maybe the only testing ground anyone has left. That the corporate world is that last remaining world of formation through its values, frameworks, performance reviews, alignment talk. But while also being a place of dissolution since these bindings are only contingent andd become, as one levels up, ways of manipulation. Ways of convincing.
