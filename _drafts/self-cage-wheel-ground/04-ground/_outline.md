# Movement Four: Ground

Three completed chapters carry the final movement:

1. [The Grammar Beneath Grammar](10-the-grammar-beneath-grammar.md): recover narrative orientation, make its claims answerable to evidence, and read the honest record without exempting its reader.
2. [The Unearned](11-the-unearned.md): distinguish worth from outcomes and grace from merit; examine practice without entitlement and empirical findings about self-related processing.
3. [The Open Hand](12-the-open-hand.md): develop releasement into action, boundaries, mutual provision, and hope that no institution can possess.

The movement restores grace, worth prior to output, and hope beyond the capacity of history to repair its losses. The [epilogue](../05-back-matter/13-the-next-morning.md) returns these questions to the fictional kitchen introduced in Chapter One.
