The essays on this site over the past several months — on [grammar](/2026-01-23-grammar-rules-all.html), on [the cage and its curtains](/2026-02-09-the-cage-and-the-argument-about-its.html), on [trauma culture](/2026-01-09-trauma-culture-white-guilt-checkmate.html), on [what Marx would say today](/2026-01-27-what-would-marx-say-today.html), on [how power uses education](/2025-12-19-how-power-weaponizes-mass-education.html), on [the epistemology of impotence](/2026-01-09-the-epistemology-of-impotence-how.html), on [political containment](/2026-01-29-the-secret-every-political-philosophy.html), on [the gaze](/2026-02-11-the-gaze.html), on [grace and the cage](/2026-02-26-grace-and-cage.html) — have all been working toward this. Each was an attempt to get one piece right. Now the pieces have a structure.

## What It Is

The book is called *Self, Cage, Wheel, Ground*. Four movements.

**Movement One: Self.** There is no pre-social self. You were not given an interior and then placed into a culture. You were formed by a tradition. Different traditions — Greek, Christian, Buddhist, Confucian — produced different interiors. Not different beliefs about the same interior. Different interiors.

The modern West inherited three specific selves from Christianity. The confessing animal: Augustine's excavation of hidden depths, minus grace. The achievement subject: medieval worth-prior-to-output, minus *imago Dei*. The identifying self: the prophetic tradition's solidarity with the excluded, minus the resurrection. Each kept the practice but lost the mechanism that made arrival possible. Confession without absolution. Labor without a floor. Solidarity without resolution.

The liberal self — the autonomous chooser who stands prior to all traditions and selects among them — is the most sophisticated trap of all. It is itself a formation. It was formed by a tradition. It just cannot see the tradition that formed it.

**Movement Two: Cage.** These broken selves were *selected*. Not designed. Not conspired. Selected by the same pressure that selects organisms: legibility. Power can only administer what it can read. The confessing animal, the achievement subject, the identifying self produce documentable interiors, measurable outputs, stable categories. The formations that don't — Eckhart's *Gelassenheit*, the Confucian relational self, the Buddhist dissolution of wanting — were selected against. Not suppressed. Outcompeted.

The cage stays up without guards. It reproduces through imitation before language. The father who cannot sit still is not delivering a lecture. He is demonstrating a relationship to time. It is formalized by school. Growth mindset teaches that bad outcomes reflect insufficient practice. Never unreasonable conditions. It is installed in the mouth through professional vocabulary that makes certain thoughts unsayable. Not forbidden. Absent from the available grammar.

**Movement Three: Wheel.** The cage is not unique. It is one instance of a pattern that has repeated across centuries with no variation. When the current cage breaks, the wheel turns to manufacture the next. Three laws explain why.

*Gravity.* Life captures more than it expends. Surplus funds structure. Structure improves capture. Advantage compounds. This precedes capitalism. Precedes humanity. It is thermodynamic.

*The remainder.* To define is to exclude. Every liberation redraws the boundary. The next revolution is built by those the last one left behind. Not conspiracy. Geometry.

*Convergence.* You are born into a network. The network holds a grammar. The grammar prizes certain goods. Everyone shaped by that grammar converges on those goods — not because they watched each other want them, but because the grammar's fixed-point subspace is the same for all of them. The post-colonial state replicates the extractive machinery of the colonial power. Not because the revolutionary imitated the oppressor. Because the field in which she learned to operate has the same attractor structure as the one she opposed.

These laws require no bad actors. A society of philosophers who understood every word of this would experience them identically.

**Movement Four: Ground.** The cage and the wheel run on fuels: accumulation, legibility, grammatical convergence, and even the refusal of all three. The question becomes: is there a desire that does not run on scarcity? A wanting whose object multiplies when more hands reach for it?

The wheel has no category for this. The mystical traditions claim it exists. The structure of the problem, followed honestly, points toward what they describe. The exit is not a technique. The ratchet digests every technique. It is grace — receiving what cannot be earned, achieved, or performed.

## The Theoretical Underpinning

Alongside the book I've been developing a formal mathematical model — [Recognition Dynamics](/2026-03-16-recognition-dynamics.html) — that gives these claims precise structure. The model treats each person as a vector in an infinite-dimensional space and each social grammar as a finite-dimensional projection.

- **The irreducibility of the Want** (Theorem 3.5). The gap between who you are and what any grammar can hold of you is strictly positive. No behavioral strategy can reduce it to zero. The cage is a cage even if the tradition built you.
- **Field formation** (Theorem 4.1). Under shared grammar, independent actors converge to a common subspace without coordination. No one needs to imitate anyone. The grammar itself is the attractor. Fields self-accelerate as they grow.
- **The inverse law** (Theorem 4.3). The larger a field grows, the worse its average recognition fidelity. The bigger the movement, the less it sees any individual member.
- **The legibility trap** (Theorem 7.4). Prior-conditioned recognition makes incremental escape from an established identity self-defeating. Gradual change is punished. Prior-revision requires a discontinuous jump.
- **Meta-awareness** (Theorem 9.5). A subject who has internalized the gap's irreducibility and reorients from signal-maximization to self-proximity is decoupled from field-formation pull and legibility-trap reinforcement simultaneously. When the disclosure optimum and trap attractor are sufficiently separated, prior-revision happens as structural consequence rather than volitional act.

The math does not prove the book's philosophical claims. It shows they have the structure of necessities, not preferences.

## How It Was Written

I drafted the book with Claude — Anthropic's AI. Over many sessions I directed the structure: which arguments to develop, which thinkers to bring in, which examples to use, how the movements connect. Claude wrote most of the draft prose under that direction. The structure is right. The arguments are in place. The examples land.

I will rewrite the whole thing in my own voice. The ideas are mine. The current sentences are not. Claude was a good thinking partner for figuring out what the book needed to be — what order the arguments should come in, which historical examples to pull, how to connect a formal mathematical model to a genealogy of the modern self to a diagnosis of political capture. That process was genuine and I don't want to hide it.

## What's Next

Movement Four is outlined but not drafted. The question it addresses — whether an exit from the wheel exists that the wheel cannot digest — is the hardest to write. The answer points toward something that resists being captured in argument. Eckhart's *Gelassenheit*. The open hand. Grace as structural necessity rather than religious preference.

Then I rewrite. The draft gave me the book. Now I need to give the book a voice.
