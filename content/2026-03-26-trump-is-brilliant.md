This'll ruffle some feathers. But all the chaos, all the flooding, all the entanglements:

- ICE deportations
- Tariffs
- Greenland
- Gulf of America
- Iran War
- Outlandish executive orders

All this looked like chaos to me for the last year.

Up until I developed my theory of "Attention, Subject, and Form."

The conclusion of that project is this:

> A subject `S` who has projected themselves into a socially legible form `F(S)` to be received by an attention field `G(A)` becomes expected to hold `F(S)`. Any deviation from `F(S)` by `S` is met with resistance, or rationalization, by `G(A)` and the subject is forced back into `F(S)`. The only possible way to break `F(S)` is through rupture.
