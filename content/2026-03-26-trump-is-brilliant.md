This looked like chaos to me for the last year.

Until I developed my theory of "Attention, Subject, and Form."

The conclusion of that project is this:

> A subject `S` who has projected themselves into a socially legible form `F(S)` to be received by an attention field `G(A)` becomes expected to hold `F(S)`. Any deviation from `F(S)` by `S` is met with resistance, or rationalization, by `G(A)` and the subject is forced back into `F(S)`. The only possible way to break an over-claimed `F(S)` is through rupture.

[[2026-03-17-S-F_of_S:In plain terms]]:

> We take on different forms to be accepted by different groups. The gamer nerd at the con. The rugged bear drinker at the brewery. Forms that we perform frequently get expectations attached to them. Your boss knows certain things about your work self: what projects you like, how late you will stay, what tasks you avoid. Sometimes we want to revise our forms. This is met with resistance or ignored: "oh, he is having a bad day" or "he's in a bad mood" or "they're going through a phase." To get the change to stick and be accepted often requires a rupture. A pattern of behavior so outside the expected norm that it finally registers. That it is powerful enough to dissolve the old expectations that were holding your old form in place. Telling your boss you'll quit unless given some new type of opportunity. Telling your wife you never want to go on a certain type of trip again.

America has had a specific form for the last 80 years. One of hegemon, regional stabilizer, lender of last resort, security blanket. This has come at significant cost to America itself although we will not debate that here. Retreating from this form is near impossible. Any minor deviation will be reversed by the pull of prior commitments. The global policy network, NGOs, foreign alliances, prior commitments.

The only way to break such a thoroughly entangled form is through complete chaos and rupture. To make America present as something completely different. A destabilizer. A cannibalizer of allies. A threat to its own interests. A threat to its own security guarantees.

**What is the new form?**

The NSS clearly lays this out. America is returning to the Monroe doctrine. America is retreating from the middle east and anywhere that is not the Western hemisphere.

> "[T]he days in which the Middle East dominated American foreign policy in both long-term planning and day-to-day execution are thankfully over." - [NSS](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-National-Security-Strategy.pdf)

The Iran war is clearly in furtherance of this goal. You cannot leave the middle east by simply saying you want to leave. All of the GCC nations where you have bases and provide security guarantees will refuse it. You can, however, start a war with Iran that restructures political economy in the region. That shuts down global oil trade. That shows that America is the real threat to stability in the region. After which your withdraw will be welcomed.

The silence of Russia and China is diagnostic. Nations whose interests are served by American retrenchment don't need to react — they just wait. Europe's alarm is equally diagnostic, and equally rational: the security architecture of the last 80 years was Europe's free lunch. The panic isn't strategic incompetence. It's the sound of a bill coming due with no way to pay it.

We shall see if Iran provides the US the off-ramp it requires to exit or if Israel drags America further in.

**But why?**

The new form is a concentration of power in the Western hemisphere _because_ of the coming climate crisis. The consensus is that the right-wing is all a bunch of climate deniers. That is true if you listen to their words. If you watch their actions, it is the opposite. The right is dominated by military thinking. And the military _knows_ climate crisis is coming. They move bases. They publish reports. They build new asset types. The plan war games around rising sea levels.

What is the one region to see minimal impact due to climate change? North America. What opens up as the world warms? Greenland. The arctic. Canada.

The US is resurrecting the monroe doctrine because we cannot be on the hook for saving the rest of the world as it turns to desert, floods, and mass migration consumes Europe.
